F1 Tutor – Windows guide
========================

1. Starting
   Double-click F1Tutor.exe. A black window opens, then your browser with the
   app. Keep the black window open while driving; closing it stops the app.
   The language can be switched in the top right corner (English / Slovenčina).

2. First start: Windows warning
   Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info", then
   "Run anyway". It appears because the app isn't signed with a paid certificate.

3. First start: firewall
   If a "Windows Defender Firewall" window appears, tick "Private networks"
   and click "Allow access". Without it, no data arrives from the game.

4. Game settings (Settings → Telemetry)
   UDP Telemetry: On
   UDP Broadcast Mode: Off
   UDP IP Address: the address shown in the black window
   UDP Port: 20777
   UDP Send Rate: 60 Hz
   UDP Format: 2025
   The computer and the console must be on the same home network.

5. Voice
   In the "Real driver" panel, click "Turn on voice". It's needed each time the
   page is opened. The cues are spoken by the Windows voice in the app's
   language.

6. Saved drives
   They're stored in the F1Tutor-data folder next to F1Tutor.exe. If you move
   F1Tutor.exe, move that folder too. To back them up, copy the folder.

7. Your own voice
   Make a folder called "voice" next to F1Tutor.exe with the files Brzda.m4a
   (brake), Zaboc.m4a (turn) and Plyn.m4a (throttle), then restart the app.
   It plays them in place of the spoken words.

If something doesn't work, take a photo of the black window and the app's top bar.
